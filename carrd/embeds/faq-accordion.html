How does the membership work?

Each month we announce a handful of new hardcovers. Members pick one (or skip the month), and we ship it to your door.

Can I skip a month?

Yes. Skip any month before the deadline and you won't be charged.

Where do you ship?

We currently ship within YOUR COUNTRY. Shipping is free on every box.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

Yes. Choose a 3, 6 or 12-month gift and we'll email the recipient on the date you pick.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from your account page. No phone calls, no fuss.
